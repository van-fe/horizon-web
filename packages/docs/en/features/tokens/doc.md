# Tokens

In `horizon-web`, we provide many `Tokens` to constrain various forms of components, including 6 major aspects: color, font size, border radius, spacing, shadow, and animation parameters.

You can also use these `Tokens` to develop in your own business system. Only when the semantics of using `Tokens` are the same, after theme replacement, will there be a consistent style form with `horizon-web`.
