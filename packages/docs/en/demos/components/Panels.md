## Vertical Direction
By default, panels have horizontal animation effects and automatically detect whether the switching direction is from left to right or from right to left, displaying the most appropriate animation. You can also enable vertical animation by setting `vertical`.
:::demo components/Panels/vertical.vue :::

## Additional Styles
To improve flexibility, panels try not to have irrelevant styles. You can add the styles you want through the utility classes in [Styles & Animation](/horizon-web/style-animation) or `style`.
:::demo components/Panels/style.vue :::
