## Basic usage
Compose resizable regions with SplitterPanel. Sizes are percentages.
:::demo components/Splitter/basic.vue :::

## Vertical, constrained, and collapsible
Panels support minimum and maximum sizes. Double click a separator or press Enter while focused to collapse and restore an eligible panel.
:::demo components/Splitter/vertical.vue :::

Separators support arrow keys. Hold Shift for five times the configured step, or use Home/End to move to a boundary.
